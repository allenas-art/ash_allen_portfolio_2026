About me

My name is Ash, and I am a year 11 student of Western Springs College. I am currently learning drums and piano and am really into music. My favourite artists are probably David Bowie and The Jam. I also play a lot of video games, some of my favourites are Bloodborne and the God of War series. I have always been interested in the stories that can be told through video games, but never imagined I could get the chance to help create those stories. My brothers are equally as passionate and now they go to MDS and are learning all kinds of skills, helping me realise that I can work towards and maybe even create my own videogames.
